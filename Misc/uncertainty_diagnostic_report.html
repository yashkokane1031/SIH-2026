Core Diagnostic Finding

The production uncertainty threshold (UNCERTAINTY_THRESHOLD = 0.15) is completely inert on real histology data. The entire 20-slide real CAMELYON16 cohort has uncertainty values strictly under 0.0866. The model is confidently wrong on held-out test micrometastases (tumor_007 at 0.0457, tumor_010 at 0.0586).

STEP 3 Proposed Strategic Options

Option A: Empirical Threshold
Fast Calibration
Set UNCERTAINTY_THRESHOLD = 0.045 in pipeline routing.
  • Pro: Catches 100% of test misses (tumor_007, tumor_010) by routing them to Uncertain.
  • Tradeoff: 60% false-flag rate (3 of 5 flagged slides are correct).
  • Downside: Directly overfits to N=2 test points on an N=20 dataset.
Option B: Probability-Zone Triage
Architectural Fix
Acknowledge MC-Dropout is uninformative at N=20. Route slides with probabilities in the ambiguous zone (e.g. 0.20 < prob < 0.70) to Pathologist Review.
  • Pro: More honest mathematically; does not rely on weak dropout noise.
  • Pro: Directly captures boundary micrometastases where feature attention is low.
  • Next Step: Requires 30–50+ real slides or 5-model deep ensemble for true uncertainty.
